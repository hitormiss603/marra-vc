About

Father, founder, investor and advisor.

I started my career at IBM's One Madison Avenue office in New York, in a consulting leadership development program. I realized big companies weren't for me, and in 2011 I joined Uber as employee #33 to launch its Boston operations.

At Uber, I launched and scaled Uber Black, TAXI, SUV and uberX in Boston, then led the Operations & Logistics Analytics and Process teams through hypergrowth from 2011 to 2013. In 2013 I launched a second marketplace, Uber Vehicle Solutions, running its operations across the East Coast and Midwest and leading negotiations with auto lenders, rental companies, dealerships and manufacturers on driver-partner offerings, reporting to the CFO.

I've been angel investing since 2016, and after my career at Uber I began advising companies in 2018. Then, after a health issue, I co-founded SunDay Drinks in 2025, a direct-to-consumer non-alcoholic beverage brand.

My investment thesis has stayed mostly the same: B2B software, marketplaces, AI and robotics. What has sharpened is that a physical, real-world product or operation powered by AI software is now close to a requirement for me.

I split my time between Portsmouth, NH and Boston, and invest alongside several Uber alumni syndicates.
